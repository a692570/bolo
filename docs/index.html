The whole product is three gestures

Nothing listens before the hold. Nothing survives the release.

  1. 01

    Hold

    The microphone opens the instant you press and closes the instant you release. Nothing before, nothing after, nothing saved.

  2. 02

    Speak

    Talk the way you actually talk. Rambles, fillers, wrong dates you correct mid-sentence. Bolo keeps up with streaming transcription.

  3. 03
    Let’s move the sync to October 15th.

    Release

    Fillers drop, self-corrections collapse, punctuation lands. Clean text pastes at your cursor and your clipboard is restored.

What’s inside

Dictation is easy. Cleanup is the product.

  • Voice transforms

    Select any text and say “Bolo, rewrite that: make it formal” or “Bolo, polish that” to rewrite in place. No dialog, no switching windows.

  • Self-correction collapse

    Say the wrong date, catch yourself, keep talking. “September 15th… actually, October” cleans down to October. It knows the difference between a correction and new information.

  • Vocabulary aliases

    Teach Bolo your recurring names once. cloud becomes Claude before the model ever sees it, and the terms you correct most get priority in transcription.

  • Per-app cleanup styles

    Email gets structure. Chat stays casual. Bind a cleanup style to Slack, Mail, Notes, or any app from the menu bar.

  • Any hotkey

    Right Option, Right Control, Right Shift, Fn, F1–F19, Caps Lock. Bind a second key to re-paste your last transcript.

  • Local history, clipboard intact

    Recent dictations stay on your Mac and are one menu-bar click away. Bolo snapshots your clipboard before pasting, then puts it back.

Voice commands

Say this. Get that.

SayBolo does
scratch that Deletes what you just dictated.
actually <replacement> Corrects what you just said, in place.
Bolo, polish that Rewrites the last dictation, cleaned up.
Bolo, prompt that Rewrites the last dictation as a prompt.
Bolo, rewrite that <instruction> Rewrites any selected text with your instruction.
paste-last hotkey Re-pastes your most recent transcript.

Install

Two minutes, one script.

macOS 12+ · Rust · Python 3 · your Telnyx API key

The script builds the binary, walks you through the hotkey picker, and registers Bolo as a login item. Grant Microphone and Accessibility when macOS asks. That’s the whole setup. 

git clone https://github.com/a692570/bolo.git
cd bolo
./install.sh

“Install Bolo from github.com/a692570/bolo on my Mac.
